Privacy Policy for Underground Mine

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Underground Mine. The game is made by Yossi Vatori, an independent developer. If you have a question, write to yossivatori@gmail.com.

What the game keeps

The game saves your progress, such as your money, upgrades, land and settings, in storage on your own device. This save is not sent to the developer. The game has no accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

The Android version asks for your year of birth once. The answer stays in your save on your device, and it is used only to decide whether ads can be shown.

Ads

The Android version shows optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward. If your year of birth means you could be under 13, the game does not load any ads, and the rest of this section does not apply. Other ads are provided by Unity Technologies through its Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads services. The game asks these services to treat every player as not having agreed to personalized ads and as having opted out of the sale of personal information. Unity's own systems still handle data in order to deliver ads.

Unity publishes the following list for its Unity Ads software. This data is collected by Unity and may be shared with its partners. It is not sent to the developer.

The web version of the game shows pretend ads only. They load no ad network and collect nothing.

Bug reports

REPORT A BUG in the options opens your own email app with a message to the developer. Besides what you write, the message holds the game version, your phone model, Android version, screen size and language, where you are in the game (land, depth, money, play time) and the game's recent error messages. The game itself sends nothing: the email goes only if you send it, from your own email account.

The developer uses these emails only to find and fix problems in the game, and deletes an email when you ask.

Your choices

  • Watching an ad is always your choice. You can play without watching any.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device's settings. On many phones this is under Settings, then Google, then Ads.
  • Some ads show a Data Privacy icon, where you can opt out of certain personalized ads.
  • To ask Unity to access or delete data it holds about you, follow the instructions in Unity's privacy policy, or write to DPO@unity3d.com.

Deleting your Underground Mine data

Your save holds your progress, upgrades, land and settings, and on Android your year of birth. It stays on your device. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing for the developer to delete or keep.

To delete your save:

  • Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. On many phones this is Settings, then Apps, then Underground Mine, then Storage, then Clear data.
  • START OVER in the game clears your progress but keeps your year of birth.
  • On the web version, clear your browser's site data.

To delete ad data held by Unity: follow the instructions in Unity's privacy policy, or write to DPO@unity3d.com. Unity decides what it keeps and for how long, as described in its privacy policy.

Security

Your save is kept in the game's own storage on your device. Unity states that the data its ad software collects is encrypted while it travels over the internet.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
